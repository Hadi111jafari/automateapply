Features

A copilot that does the work.

Most job tools give you a list. Amber runs the search, writes the resume, fills the form, and follows up — end to end.

Smart job discovery

Monitors 50+ boards, company pages, and hidden markets. Filters out listings that don't match your salary, location, or seniority bar.

✓ LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Ashby, Workday
✓ Salary range enforcement
✓ "Fresh in last 24h" feed

Resume tailoring, per job

Rewrites your resume for every role in under 5 seconds. Highlights the right bullets, swaps keywords, recalibrates your summary — all without lying.

✓ ATS keyword optimization
✓ Truthful rewrites (no fabrication)
✓ Versions saved, side-by-side diff

Auto-apply, human-quality

Handles multi-step forms, custom questions, and cover letters like a real applicant. Pauses on anything risky and asks you first.

✓ Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby
✓ Custom Q&A, generated from your story
✓ Daily caps and cooldowns

Follow-ups that land

Detects when a recruiter has gone quiet and sends a polite nudge calibrated to the company. Tracks every touchpoint in one timeline.

✓ Smart timing (never on weekends)
✓ Tone matching per industry
✓ Auto-pause on out-of-office

Interview copilot

Real-time prompts, talking points, and "don't say that" flags during calls. Post-call, generates a debrief and a thank-you note.

✓ Live audio & Zoom support
✓ Company research, generated
✓ Practice mode with mock Qs

Market intelligence

Tells you which skills are spiking in salary, which companies are hiring your role, and when to push vs. wait. The pulse of the market, daily.

✓ Salary trend graphs
✓ Competitor move tracking
✓ Weekly strategy email
How it works

From "thinking about it"
to signed offer.

01

Set your target

Upload your resume once. Tell Amber the roles you want, salary floor, locations, and what's a deal-breaker. Takes 90 seconds.

// Your preferences { "roles": ["Senior PM", "Director of Product"], "salary_min": 180000, "locations": ["Remote US", "NYC"], "deal_breakers": ["agencies", "Series A"] }
02

Amber gets to work

Every day, Amber scans 200+ sources, scores matches against your profile, tailors your resume, and submits applications. You get a single daily digest.

// 6:00 AM digest Applied: 23 new roles (92%+ match) Tailored: 23 resume versions Interview: 1 recruiter screen (Stripe) Follow-up: 4 pending nudges sent
03

You show up, Amber does the rest

When recruiters reply, Amber triages, schedules, and preps you. During the call, the interview copilot whispers the right answers. You close.

// 2:30 PM — Stripe recruiter screen Prep ready: 3 talking points, 2 questions to ask Watchlist: "AI policy experience" — highlight early Saved: 1 debrief, 1 thank-you note (drafted)
